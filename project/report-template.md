# Project Report (template)

This structure is used for the Mid-Project Report (Week 5), the Draft Report (Week 7), and the Final Project Report (Week 8). The final report is 1500 to 3000 words (roughly 5 to 10 pages with figures and references); earlier milestones may leave later sections as short status notes.

## 1. Title and team

Project title, team members, and the model family or families used.

## 2. Abstract (about 150 words)

A self-contained one-paragraph summary of the problem, approach, and main results.

## 3. Introduction (300 to 400 words)

The problem addressed, why it matters, the approach taken, and the high-level findings.

## 4. Related work

Five to ten primary citations situating the work. Brief; this is not a review article.

## 5. Methods

Data, model architecture, training procedure, and evaluation procedure. Be specific enough that a classmate could reproduce the project from this section.

## 6. Results

Quantitative and qualitative findings. Include 4 to 6 figures or tables in the final report (2 to 4 at the mid-project stage). State the evaluation set, sampling procedure, and metric implementation.

## 7. Discussion

What worked, what did not, what surprised you, and how the results compare to prior work.

## 8. Limitations and future work

An honest accounting of what the project does not do. What would you tackle with another month? Another year?

## 9. References

Any standard format.

## Code and reproducibility

Provide a link to a public repository (or a zip of notebooks) with a README that explains how to reproduce the main results: environment, random seed, and commands.
