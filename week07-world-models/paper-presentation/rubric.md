# Research Paper Presentation: Rubric

## Point breakdown (100 points)

This is the breakdown used for grading the recorded presentation.

| Criterion | Points | What earns full credit |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem framing and motivation | 25 | Clearly explains what the paper is solving and why it matters, in language a classmate from outside the sub-area can follow |
| Method explanation | 25 | Walks through the technical contribution with diagrams and equations; equations are explained, not just shown |
| Results and limitations | 20 | Presents the main results clearly and covers what the paper does not do |
| Slide quality | 15 | Readable fonts, one idea per slide, citations on borrowed figures |
| Timing, pace, and audio | 10 | Between 12 and 15 minutes; pauses give the audience time to read; audio is clear |
| Captions or transcript | 5 | Present and reviewed for accuracy |

## Descriptive performance levels

The table below describes performance levels for the main technical criteria and is used as guidance when assigning partial credit.

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Average | Below average | Poor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explain the algorithm and principles | Detailed, accurate explanation of the algorithms; math and technical details clearly presented; connects to the research method and implementation | Mostly accurate, minor gaps | Adequate explanation; some details missed; shows general understanding | Partial or surface-level understanding; lacks depth | Significant errors or omissions |
| Explanation of figures and tables | Thorough explanation of all figures and tables; interprets data and links results to claims | Most explained clearly, minor gaps | Adequate explanation but lacks depth or misses some insights | Some glossed over or vague | Ignored or misinterpreted some figures or tables |
| Critical evaluation and contribution | Balanced, insightful critique of methods and assumptions; clearly articulates novelty, impact, and relevance to generative AI | Several strengths and weaknesses identified; contribution explained with some clarity | Adequate critique; misses nuance or broader context | Generic or superficial discussion | No meaningful critique; vague on contribution |
| Preparation | Clearly well prepared; informative, structured, shows significant rehearsal | Mostly prepared; minor issues with flow or clarity | Adequately prepared; somewhat clear but uneven | Limited preparation; disorganized at times | Insufficient evidence of preparation |
