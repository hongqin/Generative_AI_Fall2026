# Recorded Final Project Presentation

*Graded discussion, 100 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is the **Recorded Final Project Presentation**, worth 100 points in the Course Project Presentation group (10% of the course grade). Same presentation skills you used for the Module 7 Recorded Paper Presentation, this time applied to your own project. The format is a graded discussion: post your presentation as a reply, then comment substantively on at least one classmate’s presentation. The peer commentary is part of the rubric.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading. Attach your video (or paste a hosted link) plus your slide PDF.

### Task

Record a **12- to 15-minute slide-based video presentation** about your own course project. Apply the same six-section structure from Module 7 Topic 4, but adapted for presenting your own work rather than a paper:

1.  **Title and the “why care” opener** (~1 min). Project title, your team, one-sentence statement of what you built.
2.  **Problem and background** (~2-3 min). What problem did your project address? What was the landscape before you started? Cite 2-3 prior works.
3.  **Methods** (~4-5 min). The longest section. Walk through what you actually did: data, model architecture, training procedure, evaluation. Show diagrams.
4.  **Results** (~2-3 min). Quantitative and qualitative findings. Show 2-4 figures.
5.  **Limitations and future directions** (~1-2 min). Honest accounting of what the project does not do; what you would tackle next.
6.  **Takeaways** (~1 min). One or two clean takeaways for the audience.

### Format requirements

- **Slide-based:** 10-20 slides. Record yourself walking through the slides; do not just talk to camera.
- **Length:** 12-15 minutes target; 16 minutes hard ceiling.
- **Recording tools:** ODU Panopto (recommended), Zoom local, Microsoft Teams, Loom, OBS, QuickTime, or your phone’s screen recorder.
- **Where to put the video:** upload directly to this discussion reply OR attach a link to a hosted recording (Panopto, OneDrive, YouTube unlisted, Loom, etc.) with view permissions for your instructor and classmates.
- **Attach your slide PDF** as a separate file on the same reply.
- **Captions or transcript required.** Use the recording tool’s auto-captions (review for accuracy) or attach a script as a separate file.

### Peer engagement (required)

Comment substantively on **at least one classmate’s presentation**. A good comment does one or more of:

- Asks a clarifying question about their methods or results.
- Suggests an improvement or extension based on something we covered in the course.
- Connects their project to a related paper, dataset, or technique that might be useful.
- Identifies a strength or weakness you noticed and explains why it stood out.

One-line replies (“great presentation!”, “nice work”) do not count toward the rubric.

### Evaluation Criteria (100 points)

- **20 pts**: Problem framing and motivation: clearly explains what your project addressed and why.
- **20 pts**: Methods explanation: walks through your architecture, data, and training with diagrams.
- **20 pts**: Results and limitations: presents the main findings clearly AND honestly covers limitations.
- **15 pts**: Slide quality: readable fonts, one-idea-per-slide structure, citations.
- **10 pts**: Timing, pace, and audio quality: between 12 and 15 minutes; pauses; clear audio.
- **5 pts**: Captions or transcript present.
- **10 pts**: Peer engagement: one substantive comment on a classmate’s presentation.
