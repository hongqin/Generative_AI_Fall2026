# Project Draft Report or Progress Report

*Graded discussion, 50 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is the third formal project milestone (50 points), after the Module 2 Proposal and the Module 5 Mid-Project Report. The Draft Report is where you show what you have built since the Mid-Project Report, demonstrate concrete results, and commit to the final deliverable shape for Module 8. Honest, specific, evidence-backed reports earn full credit. Generic “going well” reports do not.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading. Attach your 700-1200 word PDF to the reply.

### Required structure

Write a **700 to 1200 word PDF** (roughly 2 to 4 pages including figures) organized into the following six sections in this order:

1.  **Title and team.** Recap from the Mid-Project Report. Note any changes.
2.  **What you have built since the Mid-Project Report.** Concrete: new experiments, new evaluations, new analyses, new data. Specific enough for a classmate to reproduce.
3.  **Current results.** Quantitative metrics and qualitative evidence. Include 2 to 3 figures or tables. If your results have changed materially since the Mid-Project Report, show the comparison.
4.  **Gap analysis vs the Mid-Project plan.** What did the Mid-Project Report say you would have done by now? What is actually done? What slipped, and why?
5.  **Plan for Module 8 and the final deliverable.** Explicit list of what the Final Project Report will contain: which experiments will be finalized, which figures will be in the report, what the recorded final presentation will cover.
6.  **Residual risks.** What could still go wrong between now and the Final Report? Be specific and pair each risk with a mitigation.

### What earns full credit

Same principles as the Mid-Project Report:

- Show your work. Figures and tables beat sentences claiming results.
- Be specific about what is broken or unfinished.
- If you have pivoted since the Mid-Project Report, say so explicitly.
- Match evaluation metrics to your model family and data.

### Engagement

Reply to **at least one classmate’s Draft Report** with a substantive comment: a methodological suggestion, a related paper, an evaluation idea, or a question about their gap analysis.

### Evaluation Criteria (50 points)

- **12 pts:** Current results: quantitative + qualitative; 2 to 3 figures or tables.
- **10 pts:** Progress vs Mid-Project plan: honest, specific accounting.
- **10 pts:** Gap analysis: ties to particular experiments not generic difficulties.
- **8 pts:** Plan for Module 8: explicit, realistic for the remaining time, lists final-deliverable contents.
- **5 pts:** Peer reply (substantive).
- **5 pts:** Writing, organization, figures.
