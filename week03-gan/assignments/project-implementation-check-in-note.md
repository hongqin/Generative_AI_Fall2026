# Project Implementation Check-in Note

*Graded discussion, 25 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is the Module 3 informal project check-in. The proposal you committed to in Module 2 is now Week 1 old; this check-in is the natural place to share what you have actually built, what is working, and what is not. It is graded for engagement and substance, not for results. Honest reports of difficulty are valuable to the class.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading.

### What to post

Write a **short reply (around 300 to 500 words)** covering:

- **What you have built or set up so far.** Data loaded? Baseline running? Initial experiments done? Be concrete; one screenshot or a code snippet is fine if it helps.
- **What is working, and what is not.** The check-in is a place to be honest. “I cannot get the dataset to load past row 10,000” is a more useful post than “everything is going well.”
- **One concrete blocker or open question for the class.** A specific tooling problem, a methods choice you are unsure about, a paper you cannot decide whether to follow, anything that a classmate or the instructor might be able to help with.

### Engagement

Reply to **at least one classmate’s check-in** with a substantive comment: a suggestion that addresses their blocker, a related resource, a question that helps them think it through, or shared context from your own work.

### Evaluation Criteria (25 points)

- **15 pts:** Substantive initial post: covers what you have built, what is working / not working, and one concrete question or blocker.
- **10 pts:** One substantive reply to a classmate (one-line “keep going!” replies do not count).
