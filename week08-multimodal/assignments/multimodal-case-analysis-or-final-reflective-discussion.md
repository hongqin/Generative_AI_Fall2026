# Multimodal Case Analysis or Final Reflective Discussion

*Assignment, 100 points.*

## Purpose

This is the Module 8 weekly technical assignment, structured as a pick-one-of-two. Option A asks for a focused technical analysis of one current multimodal system; Option B is a personal reflection on the course as a whole. Both are 100 points and both produce a written deliverable. Pick whichever fits your project trajectory.

### Task

#### Option A: Multimodal Case Analysis

Write a **~1500-word technical analysis** of one current multimodal system. Suggested systems (pick one):

- **CLIP** (Radford et al., 2021): the canonical contrastive vision-language model.
- **DALL-E 2 or DALL-E 3**: text-to-image generation with CLIP latents + diffusion.
- **Stable Diffusion 1.x / 2.x / 3.x**: latent diffusion + CLIP text conditioning.
- **GPT-4V, Claude with vision, or Gemini**: closed-source multimodal LLMs.
- **LLaVA**: open multimodal LLM with reproducible training.
- **Flamingo**: cross-attention-based multimodal language model.
- **Whisper**: speech-to-text foundation model.
- **Sora, Veo, Movie Gen, or Hunyuan Video**: text-to-video systems.

Required structure (six sections, each ~250 words):

1.  **System overview**: what does the system do, what modalities, what is its public reputation?
2.  **Architecture**: encoder(s), shared latent space (or not), decoder, training data scale.
3.  **Training objective**: contrastive, diffusion, autoregressive, mixture; explain how the loss connects to course modules.
4.  **Strengths**: what does the system genuinely do well, with specific evidence from the paper or technical report?
5.  **Limitations**: failure modes, biases, evaluation blind spots. Cite specific examples from the paper or follow-on work.
6.  **Connection to course families**: how does this system reuse or extend the Module 2-7 material? Which model families is it composed of, and how do they interact?

Include 3 to 5 citations to primary sources.

#### Option B: Final Reflective Discussion

Write a **~1500-word personal reflection** on the course as a whole. Required structure:

1.  **How the seven families connect** (~400 words): VAE, GAN, autoregressive, diffusion, transformer, world models, multimodal. Pick the connections you find most interesting and explain why.
2.  **Your understanding before vs after the course** (~300 words): what specifically shifted in your mental model of what generative AI is?
3.  **Your project in context** (~400 words): how does your course project sit within the modern generative-AI landscape? Which families did it use? Which would you reach for next time?
4.  **One open question** (~200 words): what is one thing you would like to understand deeply that the course did not cover? What is your plan to investigate it?
5.  **One responsible-use commitment** (~200 words): pick one of the eight responsible-use items from Topic 4 (provenance, hallucination, bias, deepfakes, environment, privacy, integrity, evaluation). Describe one concrete habit or practice you will adopt going forward.

#### Deliverables (both options)

- A PDF write-up at the indicated length, submitted through this Canvas assignment.
- Optionally a supporting notebook or repository link (more common for Option A than Option B).

#### Citation requirements

Option A: 3 to 5 primary citations to the paper(s) for your chosen system. Option B: any sources you cite for the reflection (papers, books, blog posts) need full citations; minimum not specified.

### Evaluation Criteria (100 points)

- **35 pts**: Technical content: substantive, specific, evidence-backed.
- **25 pts**: Connection to course families: ties to specific modules and concepts from M2-M8.
- **20 pts**: Clarity and citations: well-written, well-organized, properly cited.
- **10 pts**: Writing quality: grammar, flow, structure.
- **10 pts**: Reflection depth (Option B) OR limitation analysis depth (Option A).
