# Orientation and Self-Introduction Discussion

*Graded discussion, 50 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is a graded discussion, but its real purpose is community. This course is fully asynchronous and online; the only way we get to know each other is through what we post. A short slide-based video introduction lets your classmates put a voice and a face to the project ideas they will read from you over the next eight weeks.

### Prompt

Record a **2 to 3 minute slide-based self-introduction video** and post it as a reply to this discussion.

Your video should cover, at minimum:

- Your name, program (CS, DASC, or other), and where you are in your degree.
- Your background that is relevant to this course: prior coursework, programming languages you are comfortable with, any prior exposure to deep learning or generative AI.
- Why this course, and what you hope to get out of it.
- A tentative direction or domain for your course project (one or two sentences; it does not need to be final).
- One non-academic thing about yourself: a hobby, a place you have lived, a side interest, anything that helps your classmates remember you.

**Format requirements**

- **Slide-based:** prepare 3 to 5 slides (Google Slides, PowerPoint, Keynote, Canva, or similar) and present them while screen-sharing or recording over the slides. A talking-head-only video does not meet this requirement.
- **Length:** target 2 to 3 minutes; 4 minutes is the hard ceiling.
- **Recording tools:** any of the following are acceptable: ODU Panopto (recommended for ODU-hosted reliability), Zoom local recording, Microsoft Teams recording, Loom, OBS, QuickTime, or your phone’s screen recorder.
- **Where to put the video:** either (a) upload the video file directly to your discussion reply, or (b) attach a link to a hosted recording (Panopto, OneDrive, YouTube unlisted, Loom, etc.); make sure the share permissions allow your classmates to view it.
- **Attach your slides** as a separate PDF on the same reply.
- **Accessibility:** turn on auto-captions in your recording tool, or include a short transcript or a one-sentence caption on each slide. Captions or an equivalent transcript are required.

### Engagement Expectations

- Post your video reply by the end of Module 1.
- Reply to **at least two classmates** with a brief, specific comment by the same deadline: something you found interesting, a project idea you would like to learn more about, or a shared background you noticed.
- Use clear, professional, respectful, and inclusive language.

*How to submit:* click **Reply** on this post; Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading. Attach the video file (or a link to your hosted recording) plus your slide PDF to that reply.

### Evaluation Criteria

Graded out of **50 points**, completion-based with light quality checks:

- **30 pts**: Video posted, slide-based, within the length limit, and covers the five required elements above.
- **10 pts**: Captions or transcript present (auto-captions count).
- **10 pts**: Two substantive replies to classmates (specific, on-topic; one-line “nice video” replies do not count).
