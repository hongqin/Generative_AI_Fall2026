# Research Paper Presentation: Guidelines

Each student records a 12- to 15-minute slide-based presentation of one primary research paper related to generative AI. The presentation is worth 10% of the course grade. The paper should be one of the three candidates proposed in the Week 1 setup note; contact the instructor before switching.

The full assignment page is [recorded-research-paper-presentation.md](../assignments/recorded-research-paper-presentation.md); the Topic 4 page [Technical Communication Through Paper Presentation](../topic4-technical-communication-through-paper-presentation.md) covers structure and slide design in depth.

## Format

- **Slide-based:** 10 to 20 slides (Google Slides, PowerPoint, Keynote, or similar). Record yourself walking through the slides.
- **Length:** 12 to 15 minutes target; 16 minutes is the hard ceiling.
- **Recording tools:** Panopto, Zoom local recording, Teams, Loom, OBS, QuickTime, or a phone screen recorder.
- **Deliverables:** the video (uploaded or a hosted link with viewing permission for the class), the slide deck as a separate PDF, and captions or a transcript.

## Six-section structure

| Section | Time | What to cover |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Title and "why care" opener | about 1 min | The paper, the authors, and one sentence on why the problem matters |
| 2. Problem and prior work | 2 to 3 min | What was the state of the art before this paper, and what gap it addresses |
| 3. The method | 4 to 5 min | The technical contribution, with diagrams and equations that are explained, not just shown |
| 4. Results | 2 to 3 min | The key empirical or theoretical findings, with the figures or tables that support them |
| 5. Limitations and open questions | 1 to 2 min | What the paper does not do; required, not optional |
| 6. Relevance to the course or your project | about 1 min | How the paper connects to a model family from the course or to your project |

## Slide design rules of thumb

- One idea per slide; a title that states the claim, not just the topic.
- Fonts readable at a distance (24 pt or larger for body text).
- Cite every borrowed figure on the slide where it appears.
- Prefer a diagram plus one equation over a wall of equations.
- Rehearse with a timer; the 12-minute floor and the 16-minute ceiling are both enforced.

## Choosing a paper

Pick a primary research paper (not a survey, blog post, or textbook chapter) with a clearly identifiable method and results. See [suggested-papers.md](suggested-papers.md) for a starting list organized by model family.
