### Part 2: Propose Your Top Three Primary Research Paper Candidates

**What is a primary research paper?** A primary research paper reports *original* work performed by the authors: a new method, a new experiment, a new dataset, a new theoretical result, or a new empirical finding. Primary papers are different from review papers, surveys, tutorials, textbook chapters, blog posts, and news articles, which describe, summarize, or synthesize work performed by other people. In machine learning and AI, primary papers typically appear at peer-reviewed venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, or as preprints on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) that are later peer reviewed.

For a general definition of primary vs. secondary sources, see [Wikipedia: Primary source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_source). ODU Libraries also publishes research guides on identifying primary research literature; please consult those if you are unsure whether a paper qualifies.
