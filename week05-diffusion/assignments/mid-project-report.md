# Mid-Project Report

*Graded discussion, 100 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is your first major formal project milestone (100 pts) since the Course Project Proposal in Module 2. The Mid-Project Report is the gate between exploration and execution: after this point, the second half of the term should execute the refined plan you commit to here. Honest, specific, evidence-backed reports earn full credit. Generic “going well” reports do not.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading. Attach your 800-1500 word PDF to the reply.

### Required structure

Write an **800 to 1500 word PDF** (roughly 3 to 5 pages including figures) organized into the following six sections in this order:

1.  **Title and team.** Recap from the Proposal. Note any changes since Module 2.
2.  **What you have built.** Concrete: data loaded, models trained (which checkpoint, what hyperparameters), evaluation metrics computed (which, on what split). Specific enough for a classmate to reproduce.
3.  **Results so far.** Quantitative metrics appropriate for your model family AND qualitative evidence (sample grids, learned representations, downstream-task plots). Include 2 to 4 figures or tables.
4.  **Gap analysis.** What is NOT yet done? What is harder than you expected? Which Proposal assumptions turned out to be wrong? Be specific.
5.  **Revised plan for Modules 6, 7, and 8.** Explicit milestones for each remaining module. The Module 7 Draft Report and the Module 8 Final Report are fixed; what will be in them?
6.  **Updated risks and mitigations.** Carry forward your Proposal risks; update based on what you have learned. Add 1 to 2 new risks. Each risk gets a one-sentence mitigation.

### What earns full credit

Topic 4 of this module has the detailed guidance on what makes a strong Mid-Project Report. The high-level version:

- Show your work. A figure beats a sentence about a figure. A table beats a paragraph claiming results were promising.
- Be specific about what is broken. “The encoder loss plateaus at epoch 12 and the latent scatter shows two clusters instead of the expected ten” beats “the model needs more tuning.”
- If you have pivoted, say so. Pivots are not penalized; surprises hidden until the Final Report are.
- Match the evaluation metric to your model family and data. Wrong metric = lost credibility.

### Engagement

Reply to **at least one classmate’s Mid-Project Report** with a substantive comment: a methodological suggestion, a related paper, an evaluation idea, or a question about their gap analysis.

### Evaluation Criteria (100 points)

- **20 pts**: What you have built: concrete, specific, with reproducibility-grade detail.
- **25 pts**: Results so far: quantitative metrics + qualitative evidence; 2 to 4 figures or tables.
- **20 pts**: Gap analysis: honest, specific, ties to particular experiments not generic difficulties.
- **15 pts**: Revised plan: realistic, mapped to specific milestones in Modules 6, 7, 8.
- **10 pts**: Updated risks and mitigations.
- **5 pts**: Peer reply (substantive).
- **5 pts**: Writing, organization, figures.
