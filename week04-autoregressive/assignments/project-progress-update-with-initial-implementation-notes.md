# Project Progress Update with Initial Implementation Notes

*Graded discussion, 25 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is the Module 4 informal project check-in, halfway between the proposal (Module 2) and the Mid-Project Report (Module 5). This is the natural place to share what your implementation looks like at this point, what experiments you have started, and where you are getting stuck. It is graded for engagement and substance, not for results.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading.

### What to post

Write a **short reply (around 300 to 500 words)** covering:

- **Where your implementation stands.** What is built and working? What are the first concrete experimental results you can show? A loss curve, a sample, a metric, a screenshot, anything specific.
- **What is not working, or what surprised you.** The check-in is the place to be honest about difficulties. A real blocker described clearly is more valuable than a vague “making progress” report.
- **One concrete next step.** What is the very next thing you plan to do, between now and the Mid-Project Report deadline in Module 5?

### Engagement

Reply to **at least one classmate’s progress update** with a substantive comment: a methodological suggestion, a relevant paper or dataset, a debugging idea, or shared context from your own work.

### Evaluation Criteria (25 points)

- **15 pts:** Substantive initial post: covers where your implementation stands, what is or is not working, and a concrete next step.
- **10 pts:** One substantive reply to a classmate (one-line “keep going!” replies do not count).
