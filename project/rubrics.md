## Week 2: Course Project Proposal (50 points)

Assignment page: [week02-vae/assignments/course-project-proposal.md](../week02-vae/assignments/course-project-proposal.md)

- **12 pts**: Problem statement: clarity, motivation, and concreteness.
- **10 pts**: Methods and data: choice justified; data source identified or a plan to find one.
- **10 pts**: Connection to one or more model families from the course.
- **8 pts**: Timeline and feasibility for an 8-week project.
- **6 pts**: Peer engagement: two substantive replies (one-line “great idea” replies do not count).
- **4 pts**: Writing, organization, references.

## Week 3: Project Implementation Check-in Note (25 points)

Assignment page: [week03-gan/assignments/project-implementation-check-in-note.md](../week03-gan/assignments/project-implementation-check-in-note.md)

- **15 pts**: Substantive initial post: covers what you have built, what is working / not working, and one concrete question or blocker.
- **10 pts**: One substantive reply to a classmate (one-line “keep going!” replies do not count).

## Week 4: Project Progress Update with Initial Implementation Notes (25 points)

Assignment page: [week04-autoregressive/assignments/project-progress-update-with-initial-implementation-notes.md](../week04-autoregressive/assignments/project-progress-update-with-initial-implementation-notes.md)

- **15 pts**: Substantive initial post: covers where your implementation stands, what is or is not working, and a concrete next step.
- **10 pts**: One substantive reply to a classmate (one-line “keep going!” replies do not count).

## Week 5: Mid-Project Report (100 points)

Assignment page: [week05-diffusion/assignments/mid-project-report.md](../week05-diffusion/assignments/mid-project-report.md)

- **20 pts**: What you have built: concrete, specific, with reproducibility-grade detail.
- **25 pts**: Results so far: quantitative metrics + qualitative evidence; 2 to 4 figures or tables.
- **20 pts**: Gap analysis: honest, specific, ties to particular experiments not generic difficulties.
- **15 pts**: Revised plan: realistic, mapped to specific milestones in Modules 6, 7, 8.
- **10 pts**: Updated risks and mitigations.
- **5 pts**: Peer reply (substantive).
- **5 pts**: Writing, organization, figures.

## Week 6: Project Methods Refinement Note (25 points)

Assignment page: [week06-transformers/assignments/project-methods-refinement-note.md](../week06-transformers/assignments/project-methods-refinement-note.md)

- **15 pts**: Substantive initial post: covers methods refined, a concrete observation, and an open question.
- **10 pts**: One substantive reply to a classmate (one-line replies do not count).

## Week 7: Project Draft Report or Progress Report (50 points)

Assignment page: [week07-world-models/assignments/project-draft-report-or-progress-report.md](../week07-world-models/assignments/project-draft-report-or-progress-report.md)

- **12 pts**: Current results: quantitative + qualitative; 2 to 3 figures or tables.
- **10 pts**: Progress vs Mid-Project plan: honest, specific accounting.
- **10 pts**: Gap analysis: ties to particular experiments not generic difficulties.
- **8 pts**: Plan for Module 8: explicit, realistic for the remaining time, lists final-deliverable contents.
- **5 pts**: Peer reply (substantive).
- **5 pts**: Writing, organization, figures.

## Week 8: Final Project Report (100 points)

Assignment page: [week08-multimodal/assignments/final-project-report.md](../week08-multimodal/assignments/final-project-report.md)

- **20 pts**: Methods section: data, model, training, evaluation; specific and reproducibility-grade.
- **25 pts**: Results section: quantitative and qualitative; 4 to 6 figures or tables; specific evaluation methodology.
- **20 pts**: Discussion: judgment, surprise, comparison to prior work; the section that distinguishes a strong report from a mediocre one.
- **10 pts**: Related work: appropriate citations; situates the project correctly.
- **10 pts**: Limitations and future work: honest, specific accounting.
- **10 pts**: Code and reproducibility: README explains how to reproduce; code runs end-to-end.
- **5 pts**: Peer reply (substantive).

## Week 8: Recorded Final Project Presentation (100 points)

Assignment page: [week08-multimodal/assignments/recorded-final-project-presentation.md](../week08-multimodal/assignments/recorded-final-project-presentation.md)

- **20 pts**: Problem framing and motivation: clearly explains what your project addressed and why.
- **20 pts**: Methods explanation: walks through your architecture, data, and training with diagrams.
- **20 pts**: Results and limitations: presents the main findings clearly AND honestly covers limitations.
- **15 pts**: Slide quality: readable fonts, one-idea-per-slide structure, citations.
- **10 pts**: Timing, pace, and audio quality: between 12 and 15 minutes; pauses; clear audio.
- **5 pts**: Captions or transcript present.
- **10 pts**: Peer engagement: one substantive comment on a classmate’s presentation.
