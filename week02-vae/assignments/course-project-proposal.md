# Course Project Proposal

*Graded discussion, 50 points.*

*Submission format in Canvas: reply to the discussion post. Your reply is the submission.*

## Purpose

This is the first **formal milestone** of your course project (40% of the course grade). The proposal commits you to a project topic, a dataset, an approach, and a timeline. You will return to and refine this document at every later milestone (mid-project in Module 5, draft in Module 7, final report in Module 8), but the direction you set here is the direction you commit to.

### Submit by replying to this discussion

**Reply to this post to submit your work.** Canvas records your reply as your submission for grading. Attach your proposal PDF to the reply.

### Required structure

Write a **500 to 1000 word PDF** (roughly 2 to 3 pages) organized into the following six sections in this order:

1.  **Project title and team.** Working title; whether you are working individually or as an instructor-approved small team (list teammates by name).
2.  **Problem statement** (1 to 2 paragraphs). What is the real problem, dataset, or research question you are addressing? Why does it matter? Who benefits if the project succeeds?
3.  **Background** (1 paragraph plus references). What is the relevant prior work, and where does your project sit relative to it? Cite 3 to 5 references (papers, textbooks, technical reports).
4.  **Approach.** Which model family or families do you anticipate using (VAE, GAN, autoregressive, diffusion, transformer, world model, multimodal, agentic)? What data will you use, and how is it accessed or licensed? What evaluation metrics will you report?
5.  **Plan and timeline.** Map your weekly milestones onto Modules 3 through 8. Be specific about what you will have done by the end of each module. The four formal project milestones (proposal now, mid-project in M5, draft in M7, final in M8) anchor the timeline.
6.  **Risks and mitigations.** List 2 to 3 concrete risks (e.g., compute budget, data availability, evaluation difficulty, ambitious scope) and a one-sentence mitigation strategy for each.

### Constraints carried forward from Module 1

- Your topic must be related to **Generative AI and/or Agentic AI**.
- **Ideally aligned with one of your three paper-presentation candidates** from the Module 1 Setup Discussion. If your project topic has diverged from all three of your paper candidates, that is fine, but say so explicitly in the proposal so we can talk about it.
- Teams must be instructor-approved *before* you finalize the proposal. Message the instructor through Canvas Inbox if you want to form a team.

### Engagement

Reply to **at least two classmates’ proposals** with one substantive comment each: a related paper, a methods question, a risk you spotted, or an evaluation strategy you would consider.

### Evaluation Criteria (50 points)

- **12 pts:** Problem statement: clarity, motivation, and concreteness.
- **10 pts:** Methods and data: choice justified; data source identified or a plan to find one.
- **10 pts:** Connection to one or more model families from the course.
- **8 pts:** Timeline and feasibility for an 8-week project.
- **6 pts:** Peer engagement: two substantive replies (one-line “great idea” replies do not count).
- **4 pts:** Writing, organization, references.
